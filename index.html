📅 6-Week Timeline

Week 1-2: Foundation

  • Hit 137-142g protein daily (non-negotiable)
  • Sunday: Batch cook chili (90 min = 5 lunches sorted)
  • Start new supplements (B-complex, Selenium)
  • Reduce Wegovy dose (drop one level)
  • Track protein in MyFitnessPal for 7 days
  • Try for 1-2 workouts
  • Focus: Building habit, hitting targets

Week 3-4: Stabilization

  • Continue hitting protein (easier now)
  • Notice: More energy, better mood
  • Aim for 2 workouts/week minimum
  • Check: Sleeping better? Libido?
  • Continue reduced Wegovy
  • Focus: Consistency, small wins

Week 5-6: Assessment

  • Continue all habits
  • Book repeat blood test (end of week 6)
  • Assess: Energy? Mood? Libido?
  • Document what's better vs. struggling
  • Decide: Need thyroid medication?
  • Focus: Evaluation & next steps
🎯 After 6 Weeks:

Best: Energy up 50-70%, mood better, libido returning, FT4 normal. Keep going!

Likely: Energy up 30-50%, mood improved, FT4 better but borderline. Consider thyroid meds.

Worst: Minimal change, FT4 still low. Definitely need levothyroxine.

🍲 Sunday Batch Cooking

High-Protein Chili (5 Lunches)

  • Ingredients: 750g mince, 2 tins kidney beans, 2 tins tomatoes, 1 onion, 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin
  • Method: Brown mince (10 min) → Add onion (5 min) → Add beans, tomatoes, spices → Simmer 60 min
  • While simmering: Boil 10 eggs (12 min), cool, peel, refrigerate for week
  • Storage: Cool chili completely, divide into 5 containers, refrigerate (lasts 5 days)
  • Daily: Microwave 1 portion chili (3 min), pour into thermos. Grab 2 eggs + cheese for afternoon.
  • Nutrition: 42g protein, 380 cal per serving
  • Cost: £8-10 total = £1.60 per lunch
